Completing the TA/native-collector requirement includes migrating the finite interval protocol into a reviewed native component before retiring the old producer.

A current list snapshot does not establish historical endpoint ownership or uninterrupted watch coverage. Cross-source joins still require explicit, trusted address-realm bindings and compatible finite observation windows; shared names or IP addresses alone do not establish identity. IP reuse, NAT, overlays, and competing owners must remain visible as ambiguity.

A usable packet or flow tuple and independently qualified routing-domain attribution are still needed to connect traffic to device context. Attachment and topology candidates do not establish a selected path or observed traversal. Multiple path and vPC alternatives must be retained until separate evidence supports a narrower conclusion.

- **Producer credential custody is not exclusive.** The `atlas-identity-producer` writes with a HEC token from the secret `atlas-identity-hec`. With RBAC now enforced, the subjects that can read secrets in that namespace are the cluster-admin group (`system:masters`, which includes the operator's admin certificate), the `admin` role bound to the front-proxy client, the built-in controllers, and the AppDynamics cluster-agent service account (`appdynamics/appdynamics-cluster-agent`), whose role grants cluster-wide secret reads. Publisher exclusivity is not established at the Kubernetes layer; it would need an index- and source-scoped token held only by the producer, a narrowed cluster-agent role, and a store-side fence.

## Decision 2026-11-01 (publisher credential hardening declined)

The operator reviewed the producer credential-custody finding above and decided not to pursue the hardening steps (index- and source-scoped HEC token, narrowing the AppDynamics cluster-agent role, a NetworkPolicy for `atlas-identity`, a store-side fence) in this lab environment. None were applied. Publisher exclusivity therefore remains unestablished, and the Node authority guard continues to report it as unqualified; this is an accepted lab limitation, not a qualification. A production deployment would need those controls or an equivalent.
