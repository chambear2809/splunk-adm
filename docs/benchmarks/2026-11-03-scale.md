# Map and table scale benchmark (2026-11-03)

Synthetic graphs from `frontend/src/scale/generate.ts`, rendered by the real
`MapView`/`TableView` in `frontend/src/scale/bench.html`, driven by
`tests/browser/perf-scale.spec.ts` (headless Chromium, 1400x900, DPR 1, Vite dev
server). Raw numbers: [2026-11-03-scale.json](2026-11-03-scale.json). "Initial"
is layout plus first render to the second frame; "pan" is a wheel pan/zoom to
the next frame (30 samples, every third a zoom). Default = level-of-detail on;
expanded = `aggregate: false` (every card drawn).

Live graphs are capped at 4,000 nodes / 10,000 relationships (top-N by
relationship, bytes, connections); the 20k-100k map rows measure the renderer
past that cap. The tenant sizing (about 2k conversation pairs per 24 h, 10x for
enterprise) sits in the 2k-20k rows. The 400k table matches a 24 h 5-tuple
drill-down. Layout alone (Node, `computeLayout`): before 25 ms at 500/2k,
104 ms at 2k/10k, 124 ms at 4k/10k, 680 ms at 4k/50k.

Finding: with a composited canvas under the SVG, the SVG's inner
`will-change: transform` layer made Chrome's Layerize step overlap-test every
paint chunk (~350 ms per pan at 500 cards). Canvas mode turns it off
(`scale/scale.css`); pans fell to ~16 ms.
