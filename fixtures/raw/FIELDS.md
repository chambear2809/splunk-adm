## Edge-case fixtures

Entities and conversations labelled `ec-` or `edge_case` in `scenario.json` exercise data-layer corner cases. Their formats follow the sections above; only the values are unusual:

- **ICMP** (`ec-icmp-orders-db`): `protoid` 1 and no `src_port`/`dest_port` fields; NetFlow ICMP records carry no L4 ports.
- **Late export** (`c-unknown-cart`): each exporter splits each direction into two records (active timeout); the second records are written to `late/stream_netflow.ndjson` and ingested after the first rollup.
- **IPv4-mapped IPv6** (`c-checkout-orders-db` in `nd_flows.ndjson`): `srcIp`/`dstIp` as full upper-case `0:0:0:0:0:FFFF:xxxx:xxxx` literals (RFC 4291 section 2.5.5.2).
- **Node replacement** (`node-c`, `node-d`): both use 10.10.20.13 with different MACs. ND reports node-c's endpoint removal as a `modType=deletion` change record, as its incremental collection reports changes (`bin/cisco_dc_nd_collector.py:757-800`).
- **ND view of an ACI endpoint** (`orders-db-01`): `cisco:dc:nd:endpoints` record with `vmName`, `tenant`, `displayEpg`, `encap` (aliased at `default/props.conf:357-371`).
- **hostNetwork pod** (`cilium-x7k2p`): `spec.hostNetwork: true` and `podIP` equal to `hostIP` (Kubernetes PodSpec); DaemonSet labels `controller-revision-hash`, `pod-template-generation`, `k8s-app`.
- **Missing cluster** (`legacy-agent-...`): pod events without the `k8s.cluster.name` HEC field, as when the chart's `clusterName` is unset.
- **Trace outside the boundary** (`batch-worker`): one SERVER span in namespace `batch`.
